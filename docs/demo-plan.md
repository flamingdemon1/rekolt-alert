# Demo plan

Owner: Manav.

This outline will be completed after the application flow exists.

## Proposed five-minute flow

1. Introduce the farmer problem and language-access goal.
2. Show one safe, prepared crop scenario.
3. Demonstrate input, clarification, and the structured preliminary result.
4. Explain the proposed safety boundary and human-review limitations.
5. Summarise the architecture, team contribution, and next steps.

The team must prepare a reliable fallback and must not imply that an unimplemented or unavailable feature is working.
